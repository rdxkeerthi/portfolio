# LUNG-NET: Unified Multimodal Lung Cancer Risk Stratification Platform

Early detection of lung nodules and accurate malignancy risk assessment are critical factors in reducing lung cancer mortality. Traditional Computer-Aided Detection (CAD) systems often rely solely on single 2D radiologic scans, ignoring discrete patient biomarkers, family history, and 3D volumetric vascular context.

**LUNG-NET** is an institutional-grade, FDA-aligned diagnostic AI platform developed in PyTorch and Streamlit. It fuses **3D isotropic pulmonary CT volumes**, **2D computer vision segmentation models**, **genomic biomarkers (EGFR, KRAS, ALK)**, and **patient exposure demographics** (Age, Smoking Pack-Years) to deliver explainable malignancy risk scoring compliant with official **Fleischner Society clinical guidelines**.

## Deep Tech Capabilities

### 1. Dual PACS Workstation & Iterative 2D Lesion Segmentation
- **Shape-Constrained Bilateral Segmenter**: Processes arbitrary 2D axial CT slices or chest radiograph inputs (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.dcm`).
- **Automatic Fallback Mechanics**: Employs adaptive contrast enhancement and morphological closing to isolate lung boundaries cleanly under non-ideal exposure, noise, or artifact conditions.
- **Lesion Target HUD**: Draws dual neon-red bounding boxes around identified nodule regions, displaying an adaptive screen-bounded HUD badge calculating total lesion surface area in mm².

### 2. Interactive 3D Volumetric Raycast Visualizer
Built on hardware-accelerated Plotly Raycast rendering, the interactive visualizer supports three distinct clinical modes:
- **Cyber-Glow**: A futuristic, high-contrast aesthetic mapping the vascular airway tree to cyan contours against a deep black void, with detected tumors highlighted in magma orange.
- **Classic Clinical PACS**: High-contrast grayscale radiologic density projection matching hospital workstation monitors.
- **Grad-CAM Explainable AI (XAI)**: Displays deep neural multi-scale feature attention weights directly overlaid onto 3D anatomical structures.

### 3. Biological Fractal Vascular Tree Generator
Rather than visualizing static, simplistic cylinder approximations, LUNG-NET implements a **recursive 3D fractal branching algorithm**. It dynamically populates pulmonary lobes with hundreds of tapering vessel sub-branches and micro-capillary meshes, mimicking true human lung anatomy.

### 4. Multimodal Biomarker Fusion & Fleischner Risk Modeling
Integrates radiomic feature extractions (spiculation, sphericity, mean Hounsfield Units) with discrete patient data:
- **Genomic Panel**: EGFR Exon 19/21, KRAS G12D, ALK Rearrangements.
- **Clinical Demographics**: Patient age, occupational exposure, smoking history (pack-years).
- **Fleischner Society Engine**: Generates automated clinical recommendations (e.g., *Repeat low-dose CT in 3-6 months* vs. *Immediate PET-CT & tissue biopsy referral*).

## PyTorch Model Implementation Concept

```python
import torch
import torch.nn as nn
import torch.nn.functional as F

class MultimodalLungRiskClassifier(nn.Module):
    """Fuses 3D CT Volumetric CNN Features with Discrete Genomic/Clinical Biomarkers."""
    
    def __init__(self, num_genomic_features=6):
        super(MultimodalLungRiskClassifier, self).__init__()
        
        # 3D CNN Trunk for CT Spatial Volumes
        self.conv3d_stage = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Conv3d(1, 32, kernel_size=3, padding=1),
            nn.BatchNorm3d(32),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.MaxPool3d(2),
            nn.Conv3d(32, 64, kernel_size=3, padding=1),
            nn.BatchNorm3d(64),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.AdaptiveAvgPool3d((4, 4, 4))
        )
        
        # Genomic & Demographic MLP Feature Extractor
        self.clinical_mlp = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(num_genomic_features, 32),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(32, 32),
            nn.ReLU()
        )
        
        # Combined Risk Head
        self.classifier = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(64 * 4 * 4 * 4 + 32, 128),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Dropout(0.3),
            nn.Linear(128, 3) # High Risk, Moderate Risk, Low Risk
        )
        
    def forward(self, ct_volume, clinical_data):
        # ct_volume: [B, 1, D, H, W]
        # clinical_data: [B, num_genomic_features]
        
        ct_feats = self.conv3d_stage(ct_volume)
        ct_feats = ct_feats.view(ct_feats.size(0), -1)
        
        clinical_feats = self.clinical_mlp(clinical_data)
        
        fused = torch.cat((ct_feats, clinical_feats), dim=1)
        logits = self.classifier(fused)
        
        return F.softmax(logits, dim=1)
```
